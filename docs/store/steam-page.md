## Short description

Steam shows it beside the capsule, and caps it at 300 characters. This one
is 186:

> Found a college and watch it grow over fifty years. Build the campus,
> hire the professors and choose each summer's class. A deep management
> game that's calm enough to play with a coffee.

## Long description

> **UniSchool** is a university management game. You found a college with
> one hall and its first program, and you grow it into a major university
> over fifty years.
>
> *"This feels like the football manager of universities …"*
> (a playtester)
>
> **Watch it grow.** Raise halls, labs, residences, venues and landmarks on
> an isometric map. Build in one of nine architectures, four of them earned
> in play, and in your own school colours. Lay out the paths and plant the
> trees. Every autumn the leaves turn, and every winter the quads go white.
>
> **Run all of it.** You teach more than 400 real courses across 42 majors,
> and each one needs a professor. Every professor is a person, with a face,
> a career and a few quirks. Their teaching and research show as numbers.
> Hire them, give them courses, and keep the good ones when a bigger
> college tries to hire them away.
>
> *"I've been looking for exactly this type of game for a long time."*
> (a playtester)
>
> **Learn it as you go.** The first five years play as chapters. Each
> chapter sets you a few objectives. Click the (?) beside one to see how,
> and **Show me** takes you to the right place. Each tab's **Guide** button
> opens its chapter again. If you'd rather find your own way, turn the
> chapters off.
>
> **One big decision a year.** Each summer you set tuition before you see
> who applies. Then you choose how many to admit, and you live with the
> class you get: its size, its quality and the beds it needs. The summer
> waits for you. The clock stops until you decide, so you can finish what
> you were doing first.
>
> **No rush.** Prestige is your college's reputation. It moves a little
> every week toward what your college has earned, and takes its biggest
> step each summer. It climbs slowly and falls faster. Money sets the pace
> in the early decades. You pay for everything up front, and the payoff
> comes the next summer.
>
> *"An alarmingly believable university simulator."*
> (a playtester on Reddit)
>
> **Fifty years, graded.** In the fiftieth summer, the Final Report grades
> your run on six axes and names what your school became. Then you can
> play on. The college keeps running, and every tenth summer adds a decade
> to the record.
>
> **Then found another.** Prestige comes from four pillars: academics,
> research, student life and athletics. You can be excellent at all four.
> Once your college stands out, the board offers you one
> specialization. You pick one of four, and it's yours for good. Your rivals specialize
> too. The next college can be different, and the architectures you've
> earned are there for it.

## Features

- **A campus that grows for fifty years,** from one hall to a university,
  on an isometric map with seasons, hills and haze around it.
- **Nine architectures, four of them earned,** and eight pairs of school
  colours. Every campus is drawn from your choices.
- **Free grounds to garden:** paths, trees, lamps, benches and flower beds.
- **More than 400 real courses** in 42 majors and seven schools. Each
  course needs a professor to teach it.
- **A faculty of named people,** with faces, careers and quirks.
- **Research** that you commission by topic, team and depth from one of
  your labs. It brings publications, breakthroughs and prizes.
- **Student life and varsity athletics:** clubs, Greek life, the playoffs,
  and 99 rival schools.
- **Learn it as you go.** The first five years play as chapters, with help
  a click away. You can turn them off.
- **The summer decision.** Set the price, see who applies, then choose
  your class. The summer waits until you're ready.
- **Prestige that moves through the year,** a little every week and a big
  step each summer.
- **One specialization, for good.** Four pillars to be excellent at, and
  one to be the very best at. The next college can be different.
- **The Final Report** at fifty years: six graded axes, a name for what
  your school became, and a report card to share.
- **Play on after the Final Report.** The run goes on past year fifty, and
  every tenth summer adds a decade to the record.
- **Sandbox mode,** from the title screen: unlimited funds, instant
  building and every building open, so you can lay out a campus without
  waiting.
- **Save slots.** You can also download a save as a file and load it
  again.
