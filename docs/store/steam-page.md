## Short description

Steam shows it beside the capsule, and caps it at 300 characters. This one
is 220:

> Found a college and run it for fifty years. Hire the faculty, build the
> campus, price each summer's class blind. Be excellent at academics,
> research, student life and athletics — but you can only be the very best
> at one.

## Long description

> **UniSchool** is a university management game about building an
> institution, from a small college with three programs into a major
> university, over fifty years.
>
> **Choose once.** Prestige is a blend of four pillars: academics,
> research, student life and athletics. A school can be excellent at all
> four. But once it stands high enough, overall or in one pillar, the board
> offers a single specialization — the faculty training program, the
> research park, the downtown and its festival, or the athletic performance
> complex — and that choice is kept for good. Your rivals specialize too.
>
> **Price the year blind.** Every summer you set tuition before you see who
> applies, then decide how much of the pool to admit, and live with the
> class those two decisions draw: its size, its quality, the beds it needs.
>
> **A campus through fifty years.** Raise halls, labs, residences, venues
> and landmarks on an isometric map in one of five architectures and your
> own school colours. Watch the trees turn in the autumn and the quads go
> white at the turn of the terms.
>
> **A faculty of people.** Every professor has a face, a career, a few
> quirks and a teaching and research record. Hire them, assign them to
> courses, and watch the good ones get poached.
>
> **A legacy, not a score.** In the fiftieth summer the Final Report grades
> the run on six axes and names what the school became. Then play on: the
> college keeps running after the report, and every tenth summer adds a
> decade to the record.
>
> Growth is slow on purpose. Prestige is graded once a year and moves toward
> what the school earned, slowly up and faster down. Money paces the first
> decades, and every commitment is paid up front while its payoff waits for
> next summer.

## Features

- **Four pillars, one specialization.** Academics, research, student life
  and athletics, and one permanent choice of the very best, offered once
  the college stands high enough, overall or in one pillar.
- **The summer decision.** Set the price, see the pool it drew, choose the
  class.
- **More than 400 real courses** in 42 majors and seven schools, each
  needing a professor to teach it.
- **A faculty of named people,** with careers, quirks and portraits.
- **Research** commissioned by topic, team and depth out of a specific lab,
  producing publications, breakthroughs and prizes.
- **Student life and varsity athletics:** clubs, Greek life, a hundred
  rival schools and the playoffs.
- **Five architectures and eight pairs of school colours.** Every campus
  is drawn from your choices.
- **Seasons on the map,** and land, hills and haze around the campus.
- **The Final Report** at fifty years: six graded axes, a name for what the
  school became, and a report card to share.
- **Play on after the Final Report.** The run goes on past year fifty, and
  every ten years the report gains an addendum for the decade.
- **Sandbox mode,** from the title screen: unlimited funds, instant
  building and every building open, to lay out a campus without waiting.
- **Saves in your browser,** with export and import.
