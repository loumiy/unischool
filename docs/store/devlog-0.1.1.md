## UniSchool 0.1.1: halls with a school, events that keep their word

Thank you to everyone who played 0.1.0 and wrote in. This update takes the
first round of what you told us, and a list of things that were bothering
us too. Your saves come with you: a 0.1.0 college opens in 0.1.1.

### A better first hour

The opening used to stop after three steps, and some of you were left
asking "now what?". It now goes on: after Founders Hall, the first
professor and the first program, it shows you how to develop a program's
next course, where to read how the students feel, and how to put that
right with a residence hall and a dining hall. Then it lets you go.

### Halls that belong to a school

A purchased hall used to belong to whichever school founded its first
program there. Now it's open until three of one school's programs are in
it, and only then does it become that school's, offering its programs and
taking its name on the map. An open hall's panel shows how close a school
is to claiming it.

And if a program isn't earning its place, you can now close it. Its
courses end and its teachers are free for others. A program that another
one needs can't be closed, and a closed one can be founded again later.
The alumni will notice.

![An open hall, a claimed one and a full one](../images/hall-claims.png)

### Events that do what they say

The events were the biggest job in this update. Too many answers promised
something the game didn't do. Now:

- **The college remembers.** Replace the boiler, and the boiler stays
  replaced. Patch it, and it will be back, and the letter will remember
  the winter you patched it.
- **Damage lands where the letter says**: a roof put off on a hall is a
  repair that hall owes.
- **People are people.** A vacant post, a visiting professor of nine
  years or a colleague's spouse can be appointed by name. The retirement
  letters name the professor who is actually retiring, down to the last of
  your founding faculty.
- **Money goes where it's given**: a campaign really launches, a booster
  club's gift goes to athletics, and some gifts endow **named funds**. A
  scholarship fund lowers the price families see; a chair pays toward a
  professor's salary.
- **Things appear on the map**: a donor's statue waits in your inbox to be
  placed, the students' worn shortcut across the lawn can be paved, and the
  ivy you leave on a building and the sinkhole you fence stay there until
  the repairs are paid.

![Ivy on a hall, and a fenced sinkhole by its steps](../images/ivy-and-sinkhole.png)

### Smaller things

- **Save slots**: three, beside the autosave.
- **Prestige** shows where it is and which way it's heading, with an arrow,
  in place of a target number.
- **A gazebo** opens when the college reaches a town's worth; the Japanese
  Garden now waits for a small city's.
- **A notice when you earn a new architecture style**, and a *New* mark on
  it when you start your next college.
- **The keyboard shortcuts** are listed in Settings.
- **The Final Report's titles** now read the standings they name, and the
  share card looks the same on every machine.
- **Fixes**: entrance steps that faced the wrong way from behind a building;
  MED550 is now Internal Medicine; ivy that looked like green splotches is
  now ivy.
