# What players said — quotes for the store pages

*Praise from Playtest 1, collected for the itch.io and Steam pages, the
trailer, devlogs and posts. Every quote is word for word from the reply
logged in [`docs/reviews/playtest-1/findings.md`](../reviews/playtest-1/findings.md),
cut only where an ellipsis shows it; the ID is the reply's there. Spelling
is the player's own unless a [bracket] says otherwise.*

## Using them

- **Attribute as "a playtester"** (or "a playtester on Reddit", "a
  Twitch streamer"), not by handle, unless the player has said yes to
  being named. Their handles are in the findings for checking, not for
  printing.
- **Don't stack one player's quotes** as if they were several people.
  R3 and R8 are one player; so are R1, I1 and I2; so are F1 and F3.
- **Mind who said it.** R9 is the composer the owner was talking to
  about the music; S1 is a playtesting service whose post was also a
  pitch, and its tester's words come from the service's AI-generated
  transcript of spoken commentary; T1's lines are from the owner's notes
  on a live stream, not a written reply. All are real, but they are weaker for a store page than
  a stranger's unprompted words.
- **Keep the quote to what was played.** Most of these are from 0.1.0
  and 0.1.1.
