## 0.1.4.1: past the Final Report

A quick fix. If you reached Year 50 and chose **Continue into the
Epilogue**, the game got stuck at that summer's admissions. The screen
said admissions was "open on its own page", but there was no page and
nothing to press. This has been broken since 0.1.3, when admissions got
its own page.

It's fixed now. If your run is stuck there, just reload your save. It
will open on the admissions page, and you can carry on into the Epilogue.

Thanks to Fonnesx, and to the player who sent it through the feedback
form, for reporting it. If something ever leaves you with nothing to
click, the **Report a bug** button in the menu is the fastest way to tell
me.

Bigger things are coming in 0.2.0: a new report card grade that rewards a
well laid-out campus, Deans who staff their own courses, painting paths in
lines, and championships you can actually win. More soon.
