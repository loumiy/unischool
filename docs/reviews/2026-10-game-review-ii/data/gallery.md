# Screen gallery (data)

Plan 86, area 2. Written by `npm run review:gallery` (`tools/review/gallery.mjs`) at commit `4062bfb`, against the production build (`vite preview`, port 4173), over 22 scenario saves and a fresh start, at 1440×900 and 390×844. The PNGs it names are working files, not committed; rerun the commands in `2-ui-and-text.md` to regenerate them.

"Words" and "controls" count the top layer: the last dialog open, else the page. A stop answered in the inbox (Plan 77C) is counted as its own card (the layer reads "Inbox › {type}"), not with the inbox list beside it, so it compares with October's modal counts. A control is a visible, enabled button, link, field or tab.

**Saves.** `founding` (week one), `y3` (year 4), `y8` (year 9), `decision` (year 3, a decision event held), `milestone` (year 3), `crisis` (year 16, the October "year 16"), `rankings` (year 16), `summer` (year 6, the first summer of the `summer` scenario), `summer1` (year 1's summer), `y25` (year 26), `ad` (year 4: the scenario now stops at the first athletic director, in year 4, not year 30 as in October), `charter` (year 7), `spec` (year 31, the specialization choice held), `summer30` (year 30's summer, Completionist), `training`, `park`, `complex`, `downtown`, `town` (years 38–40, each specialized), `y40` (year 41) and `final` (the Final Report). `demand` could not be made: the scenario runs out the clock at year 30 without a demand, as in October.

**Known artifacts.** The harness founds every college as "Test University", so the charter makes it "Test University University" in every capture after year 7. On a phone the "Faculty person" capture clicks the first tile's head; where that tile sits under the dock the click reaches the funds button and the capture is the Treasury (y8 phone). Both are the tools', not the game's.
