UniSchool · UI style studies

Four other ways the game could look

Each study reskins the same two screens: the command bar that sits along the bottom of the campus map, and the “statue donor” event from the advancement catalogue. The numbers are a year-51 save, so the only thing that changes between studies is the styling.

What the game uses today

Collegiate poster style: cream paper, the founding school colors (navy and gold here) as the chrome, thick navy outlines, hard offset shadows with no blur, pill buttons, Bricolage Grotesque over Archivo. It’s friendly and legible, but every surface has the same outline-and-pill treatment, so the HUD, panels and events all carry the same visual weight.

Study 1

Registry: bound in leather, written on laid paper

The institution as an old archive. The HUD is a tooled-leather strip with gilt lines. Events arrive as letters on laid paper, with a drop cap and a wax seal pressed in the school’s primary color. Everything leans on the fifty-year arc: the game should feel like it’s building a record.

Palette

leather gilt laid paper ink seal = school primary

Type

Cormorant SC for titles, tabs and figures. EB Garamond for body text and italic asides.

Trade-offs

  • Very atmospheric, and fits the History tab and the final report well
  • Dense tables such as Faculty and Curriculum need a sans fallback to stay readable at 12px

Study 2

Drafting Table: the campus as a set of drawings

Built around the building loop. The whole UI is blueprint line work on a grid. The HUD reads as a drawing’s title block, with ruled cells and small uppercase labels. An event is a change order with tick boxes and a red-pencil margin note. The school colors appear only in the Build stamp and in highlights.

Palette

blueprint deep line gain red pencil

Type

Archivo Narrow for titles and tabs, IBM Plex Mono for everything else, including figures.

Trade-offs

  • Unified with a map overlay mode (placing, paths, zoning)
  • A single blue ground makes school colors less present, and long event prose in mono is tiring

Study 3

Quad Bulletin: two-ink riso flyers on a corkboard

Student-life energy. Every surface is a printed sheet pinned to cork, in two riso inks with deliberate misregistration and halftone fills. The HUD is a taped-up strip, and events are flyers with tear-off tabs. This is the loudest option, and the one that most clearly says “game” rather than “management software”.

Palette

stock fluoro pink riso blue ink cork

Type

Anton for headlines, tabs and cash. Courier Prime for body text and effects.

Trade-offs

  • Strong personality that suits clubs, Greek Row and athletics
  • Overriding the school colors with fixed inks weakens the founding pick. Could swap in the school colors as the two inks.

Study 4

Front Office: a dark, dense desk in the Football Manager mould

The president’s desk. A dark, flat, information-first interface in condensed type, with small radii, hairline dividers and colour saved for meaning. The school colors shrink to a crest block and one gold accent. Events move into an inbox with a reading pane, so several can queue up without interrupting play.

Palette

desk panel accent = school secondary gain loss

Type

Barlow Condensed for headings, tabs and figures. Barlow for body text.

Trade-offs

  • Fits the most data per screen, which suits the Faculty, Curriculum and Treasury tables
  • Loses warmth and whimsy. The colorful campus map would clash unless the map gets a dusk tint.
